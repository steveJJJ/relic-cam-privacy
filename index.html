Relic Cam — Privacy Policy Application package: com.filmcam.app | Effective date: November 7, 2026 | Last updated: November 8, 2026 In one sentence: Relic Cam has no server, no account system, and does not request the INTERNET permission — your photos and settings never leave your device, because the app is technically unable to send them. 1.Introduction Relic Cam is a film/CCD-style camera application that emulates a 35 mm rangefinder and a darkroom. This policy explains what data the app uses, how it is handled, and the permissions it requests. Relic Cam is designed to process and keep your photos on your device. We do not collect, store, upload, or sell your personal information. 2.Information We Collect We do not collect any personal data, identifiers, location data, or usage analytics. The app contains no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no tracking technology. All photos and film data are processed locally and remain on your device. The app does not request the INTERNET permission. Without that permission the Android system blocks all network sockets, so the app is technically incapable of uploading your photos, developing parameters, or any other data. This is enforced by the operating system, not merely promised by us. No account or sign-in is required. The app has no user accounts, no cloud sync, and stores no credentials. The only ways data can ever leave your device are actions you start yourself in another app — opening a donation link in your browser, or sending a feedback email from your own mail client (see Sections 5 and 8). 3.Permissions and How They Are Used Camera (CAMERA) — used only to capture photos. No image is transmitted off the device. Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) — used to connect to an optional external Bluetooth flash accessory. Scanning is declared as neverForLocation and is never used to derive your location. If you never use a Bluetooth flash, these permissions stay unused. Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, Android 11 and below only) — on older Android versions the operating system requires this permission to scan for Bluetooth devices. It is restricted to these older versions via maxSdkVersion=30; on Android 12 and above it is not requested at all. Location data is never collected, stored, or transmitted, and the app does not determine your physical location. Location is not written into photo metadata. Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and below only) — used only on older versions to save photos to the public DCIM directory. On Android 10 and above the app uses MediaStore and requires no storage permission. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) and foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC) — the foreground service runs the on-device developing ("printing") job so it is not interrupted when you leave the app, and the notification only shows that job's progress. The dataSync service type is used because the job reads and writes image files; it performs no network transfer of any kind. No data is shared through notifications. Photo import — importing an existing photo uses the system file picker (Storage Access Framework). The app can read only the file you explicitly select, and requests no gallery or media-library permission. Vibration (VIBRATE) — provides haptic feedback for the shutter and controls. No data is involved. You can revoke any permission at any time in Settings → Apps → Relic Cam → Permissions. Revoking the camera permission disables shooting; revoking Bluetooth only disables the optional external flash; revoking notifications does not affect developing, only its progress display. 4.How Your Photos Are Stored Finished photos are written to the public DCIM/FilmCam/ folder through the system MediaStore, so they appear in your gallery like any other photo. They are not uploaded to any server, and we have no way to access them. Photos you import are read from the location you pick and the developed result is written back to the same folder; the original file is left untouched. System cloud backup for the application is disabled (allowBackup="false"), so app settings are not copied to Google Drive or to a new device by the operating system. Managing and backing up your photos is entirely under your control. 5.Pricing & Donations Relic Cam is a paid app, available as a one-time purchase through Google Play. Once you buy it, you own it: the app itself contains no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no advertisements, and no paid content to unlock. If you wish to support development, the About screen offers two entirely optional routes: a PayPal link that opens in your device's browser, and a WeChat reward QR code displayed as a local image for you to scan inside WeChat. Any payment is completed entirely within PayPal or WeChat under their own privacy policies. The app never sees, receives, or stores your name, card number, or any payment credential, and it is never told whether you donated. Your one-time purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment details. If a future version ever introduces additional in-app purchases, they will also be processed by Google Play Billing and this policy will be updated before that version is released. 6.Data Sharing We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer any data with third parties, because the app collects and transmits none. Two flows involve a third party, and both are started by you and fully visible to you: a donation link you choose to open (Section 5), and a feedback email you choose to send from your own mail app (Section 8). In both cases data travels directly from your device to PayPal, WeChat, or your email provider — never through the app, and never to us unless you send it. 7.Third-Party Services and Components No analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting service is integrated. The third-party services that can be reached at all are: PayPal — only if you tap the optional donation link, which opens your browser. See paypal.com/privacy. WeChat — only if you scan the optional reward QR code with WeChat on your own initiative. See wechat.com privacy policy. Google Play — for app distribution and for processing your one-time purchase via Google Play Billing. See policies.google.com/privacy. Open-source components bundled in the app are AndroidX libraries and the Coil image loader (which carries OkHttp as a dependency). They are used strictly for local UI and local image decoding: the app configures no server endpoint and issues no network request. Even if one were attempted, the missing INTERNET permission would make the operating system block it. 8.Feedback You Choose to Send Us The About screen has a "Feedback" entry. It does not send anything by itself: it opens your own email client with a pre-filled draft, and you decide whether to send it. The pre-filled draft contains the app version, device manufacturer and model, Android version and API level, screen resolution and density, and available/total memory. All of it is visible in the draft and you may edit or delete any part before sending. The app attaches no logs, no photos, and no device identifiers on its own. Whatever you choose to send is used only to diagnose the issue you reported, and you may ask us to delete it at any time using the contact address in Section 13. 9.Data Retention and Deletion Because everything the app stores lives on your device, you are always in control of it: Photos — saved in DCIM/FilmCam/. Delete them from your gallery or a file manager and they are gone permanently. Roll registry and developing settings — stored in the app's private SharedPreferences (frames remaining on each roll, per-roll grain / glow / glare / exposure values, external-flash pairing). Clearing app storage via Settings → Apps → Relic Cam → Storage → Clear data removes all of it. Uninstalling the app removes every setting it ever wrote. Note that uninstalling also discards frames still waiting to be developed. Emails you sent us — retained only as long as needed to handle your report; ask us to delete them at the address below and we will. We hold no server-side copy of anything, so there is no account or remote dataset on our side to delete. 10.Children's Privacy The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because all data remains on the device and no personal data is collected or transmitted, the app is designed to be safe for general use. It is not directed at children under 13 and contains no advertising or user-generated content features. 11.Data Security Your data never leaves your device and no personal data is transmitted, so the primary safeguard is the security of your own device. We recommend keeping your operating system up to date and using your device's lock screen. 12.Changes to This Policy If this policy is updated, the revised version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be reflected here before the corresponding app version is released. 13.Contact Us If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or want data you sent us deleted, please
